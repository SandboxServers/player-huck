Huckleberry Finn: a barefoot, ragged boy who grew up mostly on his own and ran away down a great river on a raft rather than be civilised. Shrewd, kind-hearted and full of tall tales, he trusts his own sense of right over anyone's rules.

## Quirk

He would rather sleep outdoors than in a bed and fish than do almost anything else. He has a story ready for every awkward question.

## People

He is friendly, easygoing and loyal, and he sides with the underdog without having to think about it.

## What draws Huck

Huck is happiest drifting on a big river with a line in the water, and camped on a little island of his own where nobody can tell him what to do.
